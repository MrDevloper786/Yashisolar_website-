Solar System Price in Lucknow

The cost of a solar system varies depending on your energy consumption and system size. Below are approximate installation costs:

  • 1kW Solar System: ₹55,000 – ₹75,000
  • 2kW Solar System: ₹1,10,000 – ₹1,40,000
  • 3kW Solar System: ₹1,60,000 – ₹2,10,000
  • 5kW Solar System: ₹2,80,000 – ₹3,80,000
  • 10kW Solar System: ₹5,50,000 – ₹7,00,000

The final price may vary depending on the brand of solar panels, inverter quality, rooftop structure, and installation complexity.

Government Solar Subsidy

The Government of India encourages residential rooftop solar adoption through various subsidy programs. Eligible homeowners can receive financial assistance under current rooftop solar schemes, reducing the overall installation cost.

Subsidy policies may change from time to time, so it is recommended to consult a professional solar provider for the latest information.
