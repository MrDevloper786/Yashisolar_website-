PM Surya Ghar Yojana Kya Hai?

PM Surya Ghar Yojana ek government-backed rooftop solar scheme hai jo residential consumers ko solar installation ke liye subsidy provide karti hai.

Is scheme ka primary objective renewable energy adoption ko increase karna, electricity expenses ko reduce karna aur households ko energy independent banana hai.

PM Surya Ghar Yojana Ke Benefits

  • Solar installation cost me reduction
  • Monthly electricity bills me significant savings
  • Clean and renewable energy source
  • Long-term return on investment
  • Low maintenance requirements
  • Property value increase hone ki possibility

Eligibility Criteria

PM Surya Ghar Yojana ka benefit lene ke liye applicants ko kuch eligibility conditions fulfill karni hoti hain.

  • Applicant Indian citizen hona chahiye
  • Residential property ownership hona chahiye
  • Valid electricity connection hona chahiye
  • Suitable rooftop space available hona chahiye
  • Required documents available hone chahiye

Required Documents

Application process ke liye generally following documents ki zarurat pad sakti hai:

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Electricity Bill
  • Bank Account Details
  • Property Ownership Proof
  • Passport Size Photograph

Subsidy Amount

Government time-to-time rooftop solar subsidy structure update karti rehti hai. Subsidy amount system size aur applicable government guidelines ke basis par vary kar sakta hai.

Latest subsidy details ke liye official government portal ya authorized solar installation provider se consultation lena recommended hai.

Application Process

  1. Rooftop suitability assessment karwayein
  2. Required documents prepare karein
  3. Government portal par registration complete karein
  4. Authorized solar vendor select karein
  5. System installation complete karwayein
  6. Inspection aur approval process complete karein
  7. Subsidy claim process initiate karein
